Privacy policy

Keel. Last updated: 21 October 2026.
Türkçe

Keel has no accounts, no servers, no ads and no tracking. Your expenses stay on your devices and, if you use iCloud, in your own private iCloud.

What the app stores

For each expense you add, the app saves its name, category, amount, currency, payment frequency, due date and installment details. You can optionally add a card nickname and the last 4 digits of a card. When you mark an expense as paid, a payment record is saved. The app also saves your settings, such as your home currency.

The app never asks for or stores a full card number, expiry date or security code.

Where it is stored

Your expenses are stored on your device.

If you are signed in to iCloud, the app also saves them to your own private iCloud storage (Apple’s CloudKit) to back them up and sync them between your devices. That data lives in your iCloud account. The developer cannot access it: we have no server and cannot read your iCloud data. Apple’s iCloud terms and privacy policy apply to it. You can turn iCloud off for the app in iOS Settings.

The home screen widget reads a small summary of your upcoming payments that the app keeps on your device.

Your iPhone backup (iCloud or computer) may also include app data. That depends on your Apple backup settings and is under your control.

What we do not collect

  • Name, email, phone number or any account details
  • Location, contacts, photos or similar device data
  • Advertising identifier
  • Usage analytics, crash reports or any tracking data

The app contains no advertising and no third-party analytics tools.

The one feature that uses the internet: exchange rates

When an expense is in a currency other than your home currency, the app asks the Frankfurter service (api.frankfurter.dev) for the current exchange rate. The service publishes European Central Bank reference rates. The request contains only the two currency codes, never your expense details.

As with any internet request, the service can see the IP address the request comes from. The service’s own privacy terms apply.

Device region

The app reads your device’s region setting to choose which ready-made expense suggestions to show and to pick a default home currency. This happens on your device and the information is not sent anywhere.

Purchases

Purchases and subscriptions are handled entirely by Apple’s App Store. Your card details and Apple ID never reach us. The app checks on your device, through Apple, whether Pro is active. You manage and cancel subscriptions in Settings > your name > Subscriptions.

Notifications

Payment reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device. They are not sent from a server. You can turn them off at any time in iOS Settings > Notifications. If you turn them off, the app cannot remind you.

Deleting your data

You can delete any expense inside the app. With iCloud on, deleting an expense also removes it from your other devices. Deleting the app from a device removes the app’s data on that device, but the copy in your iCloud remains until you remove it in iOS Settings > your name > iCloud > Manage Account Storage. Because we hold no data about you ourselves, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

Children

The app is not directed at children and does not collect personal data from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If a new feature changes how your data is handled, this page will be updated and the date above will change.
